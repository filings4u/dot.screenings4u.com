COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers to common platform and workflow questions.

What does the software subscription include?

The subscription provides access to the screenings4u DOT software. Testing, physicals and other fulfillment services may be purchased separately where offered.

Which DOT agencies are supported?

The platform has dedicated pages and workflows for FMCSA, FAA, FRA, FTA, PHMSA and USCG program administration.

Can a C/TPA manage multiple employers?

Yes. The C/TPA workspace is structured around employer portfolios, consortium pools, selections, testing oversight, reporting, billing and employer portal delivery.

Can employers manage their own covered workers?

Employer workspaces are designed to manage company records, covered workers, program participation and the operational records available to that account.

How are random programs organized?

Random program workflows connect covered-worker eligibility, pool membership, selection events and resulting testing activity.

Are testing services included in the monthly software price?

No. The monthly plan price is for software access. Where offered, testing and other orderable services are priced separately.

Does screenings4u DOT replace legal or regulatory advice?

No. The software helps organize operational workflows and records. Organizations remain responsible for understanding and meeting the rules that apply to their program.

Where should I start if I am unsure which plan I need?

Start with the DOT agency page that applies to your workforce. Each agency page includes its plan comparison, add-ons and available services.
