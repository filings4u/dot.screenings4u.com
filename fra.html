FRA REGULATORY OVERVIEW

FRA Part 219 reaches multiple railroad crafts and regulated-service functions.

FRA drug and alcohol requirements are centered in 49 CFR Part 219 and use DOT-wide procedures in 49 CFR Part 40. The regulated population includes covered-service employees and has expanded to roadway workers and defined mechanical employees, including qualifying contractors.

FRA Drug and Alcohol Program ↗
REGULATED SERVICE

Hours-of-service covered employees

FRA identifies train and engine service employees, dispatching employees who issue mandatory directives, and signal employees among the employees performing regulated service.

ADDITIONAL COVERAGE

Roadway workers and mechanical employees

FRA guidance also includes maintenance-of-way roadway workers and employees who perform specified mechanical tests or inspections on railroad rolling equipment.

CONTRACTORS

Contracted regulated service matters too

Contracted employees performing regulated service can fall within Part 219, which means railroads and contractors need clear records of who is inside each regulated program.

REPORTING & RECORDS

Program activity must be supportable

FRA provides compliance plans, post-accident resources, and MIS reporting requirements for organizations meeting the applicable thresholds, making organized program records essential.
