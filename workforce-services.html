Workforce Services

DOT and non-DOT capabilities. One Workforce platform.

Manage regulated and non-regulated workforce programs together. Services and features are available according to your Workforce plan and account permissions, without a separate non-DOT subscription.

Drug & Alcohol Testing

Coordinate DOT-regulated and workplace testing using a connected service workflow.

  • DOT drug and alcohol testing
  • Non-DOT urine, oral fluid, hair and alcohol options
  • Pre-employment, random, post-accident and reasonable-suspicion testing
  • Collection coordination, results and documentation

Background Screening

Keep hiring and screening workflows organized in the Workforce environment.

  • Pre-employment background screening
  • Screening requests and status tracking
  • Applicant and employee records
  • Access-controlled screening information

Occupational Health

Coordinate workforce health services and related records in one place.

  • Occupational health service coordination
  • Employer-focused exam and screening workflows
  • Appointments and service requests
  • Secure records and reporting

Programs & Compliance

Keep workforce management, testing activity and regulatory requirements connected.

  • DOT agency-specific requirements
  • Non-DOT workplace programs
  • Consortium and random-program management where applicable
  • Notifications, documents, audit history and reporting
