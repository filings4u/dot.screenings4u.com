PHMSA REGULATORY OVERVIEW

PHMSA Part 199 starts with identifying covered functions and the people who perform them.

PHMSA drug and alcohol requirements are in 49 CFR Part 199 and use testing procedures in 49 CFR Part 40. Part 199 applies to operators of pipeline facilities subject to Parts 192, 193, and 195 and to covered employees performing regulated operations, maintenance, or emergency-response functions.

PHMSA Drug & Alcohol Testing Overview ↗PHMSA Part 199 ↗
COVERED OPERATIONS

Parts 192, 193, and 195

PHMSA identifies operators of gas pipelines, LNG facilities, hazardous-liquid pipelines, and carbon-dioxide pipeline facilities within the scope of Part 199 when covered functions are performed.

COVERED EMPLOYEES

Employees and contractors

A covered employee includes a person performing a covered function, including operator employees, contractors engaged by operators, and contractor employees.

WRITTEN PROGRAMS

Anti-drug and alcohol misuse plans

PHMSA requires operators to maintain a written anti-drug plan and alcohol misuse plan, or a combined plan, describing how the operator implements Part 199 and Part 40.

TESTING

Different drug and alcohol testing categories

PHMSA identifies drug testing for pre-employment, post-accident, random, reasonable cause, return-to-duty, and follow-up, while alcohol testing has its own required categories under Subpart C.
