1. What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy describes cookies, local storage, session storage and similar technologies used on the screenings4u DOT marketing site, checkout and DOT portals.

2. Essential website and portal storage

Our portals use browser storage that is necessary for authentication, selected workspace or membership routing, support context and short-lived branding cache. These technologies help keep users signed in, route users to the correct authorized portal and maintain expected portal behavior.

3. Checkout and third-party technologies

Our checkout loads Stripe for payment processing. Stripe may set or read cookies and similar technologies needed for fraud prevention, payment security and checkout functionality. The marketing site loads Google Fonts, which may cause the browser to request font resources from Google-hosted domains.

4. Analytics and advertising

In the marketing-site files reviewed for this policy, we did not identify Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity or similar advertising/behavioral tracking scripts. If we add non-essential analytics or advertising technologies later, we will update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate consent controls.

5. Managing browser storage

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies and local storage. Blocking essential authentication or checkout storage may prevent portal sign-in, workspace routing or payment functionality from working correctly.

6. Security technologies

We may use network, firewall, bot-management or challenge technologies at the hosting or DNS edge to protect login and other sensitive pages. These services may use short-lived security cookies or request data to distinguish legitimate users from abusive traffic. Such technologies are used for security rather than behavioral advertising.

7. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when technologies or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
