FTA REGULATORY OVERVIEW

FTA compliance follows the funding relationship and the safety-sensitive function.

FTA drug and alcohol requirements are in 49 CFR Part 655, with testing procedures governed by 49 CFR Part 40. FTA rules apply to specified recipients and subrecipients of federal transit funds and extend to contractors performing safety-sensitive functions.

FTA Drug & Alcohol Program ↗FTA Part 655 Rules & Notices ↗
APPLICABILITY

Recipients, subrecipients, and contractors

FTA identifies recipients and subrecipients of Urbanized Area (§5307), Capital Investment Grant (§5309), and Rural Area (§5311) funds, along with contractors and subcontractors performing covered safety-sensitive work.

SAFETY-SENSITIVE WORK

Coverage follows the function

The testing obligation is tied to employees who perform safety-sensitive transit functions, so organizations need to know who is actually performing covered work.

TESTING PROGRAM

Part 655 testing and Part 40 procedures

FTA programs include the required testing categories and must use DOT procedures in Part 40 for collections, laboratory/MRO processes, alcohol testing, and return-to-duty requirements.

PROGRAM RECORDS

Contractor oversight still needs evidence

Transit agencies need records showing covered employees, contractor relationships, random testing administration, test events, policy implementation, and reporting activity.
