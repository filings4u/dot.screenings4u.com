FMCSA REGULATORY OVERVIEW

What an FMCSA drug and alcohol program requires you to manage.

FMCSA-regulated employers operate under 49 CFR Part 382, while DOT-wide testing procedures are in 49 CFR Part 40. The rule set affects who is covered, when testing is required, how violations are handled, and the records an employer must be able to produce.

FMCSA testing requirements ↗FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Testing ↗
COVERAGE

CDL drivers in regulated CMV operations

Part 382 applies to drivers who operate commercial motor vehicles that require a commercial driver’s license. Employers must know which drivers belong in the regulated program and keep their status current.

TESTING

Required testing events

FMCSA identifies pre-employment controlled-substance testing, random testing, post-accident testing when the regulatory criteria are met, reasonable-suspicion testing, return-to-duty testing, and follow-up testing.

ELIGIBILITY

Negative result before safety-sensitive work

A driver generally cannot perform regulated driving duties until the employer has the required negative pre-employment controlled-substance result or a permitted exception has been properly verified.

PROGRAM CONTROL

Records, selections, and follow-through

Employers need a defensible trail showing program participation, random-pool eligibility, selections, testing activity, results status, return-to-duty activity, and supporting documents.

Important: This overview is educational and operational, not legal advice. Use the linked agency sources and the current regulations for official requirements and interpretations.
