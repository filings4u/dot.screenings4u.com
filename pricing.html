Pricing & Software FAQ

Answers based on your active screenings4u catalog.

These questions use published FAQs plus the active plan, service, and add-on records in screenings4u Enterprise.

What are the current DOT Employer software prices?

DOT Employer Essential is $85/month, Professional is $145/month, and Enterprise is $245/month. Each higher tier adds additional operational and administrative services.

What are the current FMCSA Owner-Operator prices?

Owner-Operator Essential is $74.95/year, Plus is $125.95/year, and Complete is $149.95/year. All FMCSA Owner-Operator plans are annual 12-month plans for a single-driver workflow.

What are the current DOT C/TPA prices?

DOT C/TPA Essential is $125/month, Professional is $225/month, and Enterprise is $375/month. The C/TPA plans add employer-client management, consortium pools, client invoicing, and expanded portal administration as tiers increase.

Is drug and alcohol testing included in the software plan price?

Testing and other fulfillment services may be purchased separately where offered unless the selected plan specifically states that a test is included. Employer and C/TPA subscriptions are monthly; Owner-Operator plans are annual.

How do I order a drug or alcohol test?

Choose the service you need and continue through checkout. After the order is confirmed, screenings4u provides the information needed to complete the test. If you are unsure which service applies, contact the screenings4u team before ordering.

Do you have collection sites nationwide?

Yes. screenings4u coordinates testing through a nationwide collection-site network. Site availability varies by service and ZIP code, so the assigned or available location is confirmed during ordering and scheduling.

How long do laboratory drug-test results take?

Many negative laboratory drug-test results are available within approximately 2–3 business days after the laboratory receives the specimen. Non-negative results, confirmatory testing, Medical Review Officer review, shipping, weekends, holidays, or incomplete information can extend that timeline.

Can I add more employee or driver capacity?

Yes, where eligible. Additional Employee / Driver Capacity is an active add-on for supported Essential and Professional configurations. The Enterprise tier uses its own expanded-capacity configuration.

Can one Employer account manage more than one DOT agency?

Yes. The active add-on catalog includes an Additional DOT Agency Portal for supported DOT agencies: FMCSA, FAA, FRA, FTA, PHMSA, and USCG.

Can Workforce DOT connect to another system or API?

Yes. Custom API / Third-Party Connector work is an active add-on for eligible configurations. Professional and Enterprise plans have broader integration eligibility than Essential plans.

Do you offer implementation or data migration help?

Yes. Implementation / Data Migration is an active one-time service for onboarding, implementation assistance, and supported data migration work.

How can I get help with an order or result?

Call (773) 245-7009 or use the contact page. For privacy and security, sensitive test results or identification documents should only be shared through an approved secure method.
